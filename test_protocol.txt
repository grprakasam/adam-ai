Clinical Trial Protocol - ADAM-001
========================================

Study Title: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study of Drug X in Patients with Disease Y

Study ID: ADAM-001
Protocol Version: 2.0
Date: November 29, 2025

STUDY OVERVIEW
--------------
This is a Phase 3 clinical trial evaluating the efficacy and safety of Drug X compared to placebo in adult patients diagnosed with Disease Y.

Primary Objective:
To evaluate the efficacy of Drug X in reducing symptoms of Disease Y compared to placebo.

Primary Endpoint:
Change from baseline in Disease Y Symptom Score at Week 12.

STUDY POPULATION
-----------------
Target Enrollment: 300 patients

Inclusion Criteria:
- Adults aged 18-65 years
- Confirmed diagnosis of Disease Y
- Disease Y Symptom Score >= 15 at baseline
- Willing to provide informed consent

Exclusion Criteria:
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women
- Severe hepatic impairment
- History of cardiovascular disease
- Use of prohibited medications within 30 days

STUDY DESIGN
------------
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study with two parallel treatment arms:

Arm 1: Drug X 50mg daily for 12 weeks (N=150)
Arm 2: Placebo daily for 12 weeks (N=150)

Randomization will be 1:1 using an Interactive Web Response System (IWRS).

STUDY TIMELINE
--------------
Screening Period: Up to 28 days
Treatment Period: 12 weeks
Follow-up Period: 4 weeks
Total Study Duration: Approximately 20 weeks per patient

EFFICACY ASSESSMENTS
--------------------
Primary efficacy endpoint will be assessed using the Disease Y Symptom Score (DYSS), a validated 0-50 scale where higher scores indicate worse symptoms.

Assessments will be conducted at:
- Baseline (Day 1)
- Week 4
- Week 8
- Week 12 (Primary endpoint)
- Week 16 (Follow-up)

SAFETY ASSESSMENTS
------------------
Safety will be monitored throughout the study through:
- Adverse event reporting
- Laboratory tests (hematology, chemistry, urinalysis)
- Vital signs (blood pressure, heart rate, temperature)
- Physical examinations
- ECG monitoring

STATISTICAL ANALYSIS
--------------------
The primary analysis will compare change from baseline in DYSS at Week 12 between Drug X and placebo groups using ANCOVA with baseline DYSS as a covariate.

Sample size was calculated to provide 90% power to detect a mean difference of 5 points in DYSS change with a two-sided alpha of 0.05.

DATA MANAGEMENT
---------------
All study data will be collected using electronic case report forms (eCRF) and stored in a validated clinical trial database. Data will be reviewed for quality and consistency throughout the study.

Source documents will be maintained at each study site and will be available for monitoring and audit.
