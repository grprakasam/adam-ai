# Notice

The CSV files in this folder are format conversions (R `.rda` to CSV) of
datasets from:

* pharmaversesdtm v1.5.0 - https://github.com/pharmaverse/pharmaversesdtm
* pharmaverseadam v1.4.0 - https://github.com/pharmaverse/pharmaverseadam

Both are licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) and derive from the public CDISC
pilot study (CDISCPILOT01). No changes were made to values; dates were written
as ISO 8601 strings. Source URLs and SHA-256 checksums: `manifest.json`.
